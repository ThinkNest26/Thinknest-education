ABOUT US

Made by educators, for every part of school life.

ThinkNest Education was founded by three educators who wanted better tools for the schools they know. We build platforms for leaders, teachers, children and families that are simple to use and work together.

Our vision

Every child loves learning, and every school has the tools to help each child grow.

Our mission

To build joyful, easy-to-use platforms that connect leaders, teachers, children and families, so teachers spend their time teaching and every child’s progress is seen.

Our platforms

Each app works on its own. Together, they cover a child’s day from the classroom to home, and a school’s year from planning to report cards.

For school leaders and teachers

Nestopia
The complete school system
nestopia.school

Everything a school runs on, in one place: planning, assessments, attendance, report cards and calendars, with a parent portal and Nestlings, the student portal. GrowNest and TwigNest open right inside it.

Unit and lesson planner tied to curriculum coverage
Report cards with leader checks before parents see them
Parent portal and the Nestlings student portal
Each school uploads its own curriculum

GrowNest
Observations and appraisals

Learning walks, formal observations and appraisals on the MOE six-point scale. Teachers get their feedback by email, with no login needed.

Part of Nestopia, or on its own at grownest-teacher.com

TwigNest
Student data and progress

One place for every student’s data, from baseline to the end of the year. See attainment, progress and curriculum coverage, with next steps for every child.
