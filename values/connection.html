Core value · Connection

When school and home speak the same language

Children do best when the adults around them are working together. Connection means making that easy.

What connection means to us

For us, connection means parents knowing what their child is learning, teachers seeing the full picture of each child, and leaders seeing the whole school, all without anyone chasing information.

What the research says

The Education Endowment Foundation’s guidance report Working with Parents to Support Children’s Learning notes that parental engagement is consistently linked with better outcomes, and its Teaching and Learning Toolkit estimates that effective parental engagement can add around three months of progress over a year. The report gives schools four practical recommendations, with a focus on families who may need extra support, and advises schools to plan and check their parental engagement work carefully.

How it shows up in our platforms

The Nestopia parent portal and Nestlings student portal let parents see what their child posts and completes. The Maths Quest parent view shows parents their child’s progress in plain language. Spelling Bee brings families into class rounds and the Grand Final.

A tip for your school

Make every message to parents answer one question: “What can I do at home this week to help?” Specific, small actions build engagement far better than general updates.
